Typeface history. IBM Plex was replaced on 2026-09-07 because it had become a common default in AI-generated and templated sites. Newsreader / Public Sans, Google Sans Flex, Roboto Slab / Roboto Mono and then the Caveat Brush / Nunito pair followed on 2026-09-10 as the site was rebuilt as an illustrated homepage. On 2026-10-15 the site returned to Roboto Mono (headings, navigation, labels) over Roboto Slab (text), with an Aegean palette of white (#F4F4F4) and blue (#007BA7); the Caveat Brush and Nunito files and licences were removed from `public/fonts/`.

No font from the supplied Apple material was used. Apple's San Francisco typefaces are proprietary and were not considered.
