Typeface history. IBM Plex was replaced on 2026-09-07 because it had become a common default in AI-generated and templated sites. Its replacements — Newsreader (headings) and Public Sans (text) — were replaced on 2026-09-10 by Google Sans Flex, and later the same day, when the site was rebuilt in the style of an illustrated academic homepage, by Roboto Slab / Roboto Mono and then by the Caveat Brush / Nunito pair (a hand-lettered heading face over a rounded text face, chosen at the owner's request for a warmer, less formal look); the earlier files and licences were removed from `public/fonts/`.

No font from the supplied Apple material was used. Apple's San Francisco typefaces are proprietary and were not considered.
