## Question

Communities do not always respond smoothly to pressure. Under warming and fishing, a Mediterranean fish or macroinvertebrate community may reorganise gradually, or it may jump between alternative states. The project asked which of these two pictures the data support, and how climate and fishing act together.

## System

Mediterranean fish and macroinvertebrate communities exposed to the combined pressures of climate change and fishing, studied at UMR MARBEC (Montpellier) during the research phase of my MSc in Applied Ecological and Evolutionary Sciences.

## Approach

The analysis treated community change as a dynamical-systems problem. Discontinuous transitions were modelled with catastrophe theory, where a fold in the equilibrium surface produces abrupt jumps and hysteresis as control variables (here, climate and fishing) change. Stochastic models were used to represent variability around those equilibria, and effects of the pressures were estimated statistically.

## Outcome

The work was presented as an oral contribution at the 16th Colloquium of the Association Française d'Halieutique (Sète, June 2024), the only MSc project selected for an oral slot at that meeting.
