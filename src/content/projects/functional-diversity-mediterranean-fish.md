## Question

A community can lose species without losing functions, or lose functions while species counts stay flat. Functional diversity asks how much of the trait space a community occupies. These internships asked how Mediterranean fish communities fill that space and how the occupied volume responds to global change.

## Approach

Fish community diversity was quantified with n-dimensional hypervolume methods, which estimate the volume and overlap of communities in a multivariate trait space. Because trait data are incomplete for many species, missing traits were imputed using phylogenetic information, which required phylogenetic inference for the species pool. An earlier Erasmus internship (2021) analysed functional diversity under global change with statistical methods.
