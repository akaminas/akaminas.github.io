SDSN Youth is a programme of the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, and SDSN Youth Greece is its national network in Greece. The network mobilises young people to help realise the Sustainable Development Goals; its projects focus on working with stakeholders so that young people can design sustainable solutions. Its host institutions include the [Political Economy of Sustainable Development Lab](/work/pesd-lab/) at the University of Athens.

I joined in November 2023 as a sustainability research fellow, managing sustainability projects and co-authoring publications on the Sustainable Development Goals. Since May 2025 I have been the network coordinator: I coordinate its collaborations and projects on the Sustainable Development Goals.
