## Question

Sustainability targets are set for organisations, but organisations are themselves systems of people, routines and external pressures. The question is which of those internal and external forces determine whether an organisation actually commits to sustainable development and circular-economy principles.

## System

Non-governmental organisations in Greece, studied through the lenses of organisational culture, institutional isomorphism (the pressures that make organisations resemble one another) and systems frameworks that treat the organisation and its environment as coupled.

## Result

The analysis was published in 2026 in *Circular Economy and Sustainability* (Alevizos, Kaminas, Vrouvaki & Papandreou), open access under CC BY 4.0.
