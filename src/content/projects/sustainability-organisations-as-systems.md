With Antonios Alevizos, Christianna Vrouvaki and Andreas Papandreou I co-wrote a paper on organisational commitment to sustainable development and circular-economy principles among Greek non-governmental organisations. It was published in 2026 in *Circular Economy and Sustainability* (open access, CC BY 4.0). The analysis draws on organisational culture, institutional isomorphism (the pressures that make organisations resemble one another) and systems frameworks that treat the organisation and its environment as coupled.

Since 2025 I have been a research associate at the Political Economy of Sustainable Development Lab (University of Athens), working on climate-change reporting, data visualisation and scientific storytelling. At SDSN Youth Greece I coordinate the network's collaborations and projects on the Sustainable Development Goals (network coordinator since 2025; sustainability research fellow 2023–2025), and I contributed research, writing and dissemination to SDG4U, an EU-funded higher-education project on Sustainable Development Goal 4 (2024–2025). Apart from the paper, this is policy and outreach work rather than research output.
