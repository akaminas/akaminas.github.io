The lab studies the political economy of sustainable development: how institutions, the political environment and the economy interact and influence each other, and so form the conditions that allow or prevent sustainable development. It is the hosting institution of SDSN Greece and one of the hosts of [SDSN Youth Greece](/work/sdsn-youth-greece/).

Since January 2025 I have been a research associate at the lab, working on climate-change reporting, data visualisation and scientific storytelling. My work on organisations and sustainability is described under [Organisations, institutions and sustainability](/work/sustainability-organisations-as-systems/).
