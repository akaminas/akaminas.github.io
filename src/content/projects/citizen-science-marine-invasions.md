## Question

Non-indigenous species spread faster than formal surveys can follow. The projects asked whether observations contributed by divers, fishers and the public, checked by taxonomists, can fill the gaps: first records, range expansions and population densities.

## Work

Between 2018 and 2023, as a volunteer research fellow with the environmental organisation iSea, I monitored invasive species and elasmobranchs and contributed to three citizen-science and public-engagement programmes: *Is it Alien to you? Share it!!!* (public engagement and alien-species monitoring under a research permit from the Greek Ministry of Environment), the marine alien-species population-density project for Greece and Cyprus, and the MECO collaborative network for elasmobranch sightings across the Mediterranean.

A research internship at the Hellenic Centre for Marine Research (2020) on the taxonomy, stomach contents and diet indices of commercial fish contributed to a peer-reviewed paper on the feeding habits of *Dentex maroccanus*.

## Outputs

Seven peer-reviewed articles (2020–2023), including a first-author paper reporting *Phyllorhiza punctata* in Cyprus, and two conference posters at HydroMediT 2021. This is data-gathering and monitoring work rather than modelling; it is the empirical grounding for my later work on marine communities.
