**SDG4U**, *Engaging SDGs for Transformative Education and Enhanced Sustainability in Universities*, is funded by the EU's Erasmus+ programme. It works on embedding sustainability across teaching, research, operations and community outreach in higher education, including an online mapping tool for SDG initiatives. In 2024–2025 I contributed research, writing and dissemination.

In autumn 2021 an **Erasmus research internship** took me to UMR MARBEC in Sète, where I analysed functional diversity under global change with statistical methods; see [Functional diversity of Mediterranean fish communities](/work/functional-diversity-mediterranean-fish/).

Since October 2025 my PhD at the University of Groningen has been funded by an **EVOLVE fellowship**, part of the EU's MSCA COFUND scheme; see [The evolutionary consequences of isolation](/work/evolutionary-consequences-of-isolation/).
