Supervised the design and analysis of a quantitative eco-evolutionary model in which the trait distributions of interacting populations, the structure of their mutualistic interactions, population dynamics and evolutionary change are coupled. Supervision covered model formulation, implementation, numerical experiments and the interpretation of results.
